Agent 2B is now **ZimAI**: Unlimited AI for Small Jobs. Hit your usage limit? Keep going.

### What's new

- A new name, a new icon, and a brighter home page with softly drifting glows. On Windows 11 the title bars match the app.
- **CPU mode:** no NVIDIA GPU? ZimAI runs on the CPU with 16 GB of RAM, slower but it works. The **Use GPU** switch keeps your GPU free for a game.
- The model's full 128k context on a GPU (64k on the CPU).
- The server starts when you click **Start**, not by itself.
- The first message no longer stalls on PCs where part of the model is read from disk.
- The terminal agent answers from web search results, always asks before running a command, and refuses to delete anything outside your project.

### Get it

Download `ZimAI-portable.zip` below, unzip it anywhere, and double-click `ZimAI.exe`. You need Windows 10 or 11, 16 GB of RAM and 13 GB of free disk; an NVIDIA GPU with 6 GB of video memory or more makes it much faster.

**Coming from Agent 2B?** Unzip into a new folder and copy the old `data` folder next to the new `ZimAI.exe`, so you keep the model (no 12 GB download) and your chats.
